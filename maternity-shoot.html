Good to Know

Common questions.

What's the best time in pregnancy to shoot?

+

Most couples shoot between 28–34 weeks, when the bump is well defined but you're still comfortable moving around for an hour or two.

Can we shoot at home?

+

Yes — home shoots are popular for maternity sessions. We can also suggest outdoor or studio locations if you'd prefer.

Can partners and kids join the shoot?

+

Absolutely — most of our maternity sessions include a few frames with partners, siblings and family.
