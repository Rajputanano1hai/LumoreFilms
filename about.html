Our Story

Founded on one idea — tell it real.

Lumore Films is a Delhi-based wedding photography and videography studio. We started with a simple frustration: too many wedding films looked staged, and too many photographers were more visible than the moments they were meant to capture.

So we built a team around the opposite instinct — stay close, stay quiet, and let real emotion carry the frame. Years on, that instinct is still the core of every wedding, pre-wedding, maternity and birthday shoot we take on.
