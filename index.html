About Lumore Films

A Delhi studio built around real stories.

Lumore Films began with a simple idea — that a wedding film should feel like the day itself, not a highlight reel. Today our team of photographers, cinematographers and editors travels across India to document weddings with warmth, discretion and a genuinely cinematic finish.

  • Experienced photography & film crew
  • Same-team coverage from getting-ready to reception
  • Drone, candid and traditional coverage included
