FAQ

Before you enquire.

How far in advance should we book?

+

For weddings, 3–6 months ahead is ideal, especially in peak wedding season. Pre-wedding, birthday and maternity shoots can usually be booked with a few weeks' notice.

Do you travel outside Delhi?

+

Yes — we regularly shoot weddings across India. Travel and stay are added to your quote based on the destination.

How long until we receive our photos?

+

A preview gallery is shared within a week, with the full edited album and film delivered within 4–6 weeks.

Is a deposit required to book?

+

Yes, a booking deposit confirms your date on our calendar. The balance is due closer to or on the day of the shoot.
